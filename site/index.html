Seaglass

Lossless music streaming client for Windows, Android and Linux.

Unofficial client, not affiliated with TIDAL. A valid TIDAL subscription is required.

Download

Bit-perfect playback

Lossless and hi-res streams played by mpv on the desktop, with exclusive mode on Windows and the signal path shown from the stream to the output device.

A custom interface

Several themes, each with its own look, a "Now playing" view, keyboard and global shortcuts, and a home page you can arrange.

Listen together

Jam sessions on the local network or online, remote control from a phone, and playback handoff between the computer and the Android app.

Streaming only

No audio stream is downloaded or written to disk, and DRM-protected streams are not played. Free software, GPL-3.0.
