# Themes

Seaglass implementation notes, read on demand (index in [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md)). A new theme starts with its tokens in `app.css`.

## Basics

- **Themes**: `data-theme` on `<html>`, CSS tokens in `app.css`. Seaglass: translucent panels over the blurred cover (`Ambient.svelte`); Studio: flat, sharp corners, full signal path in the bar; Atelier: light, serif titles. The first three have their variants in the components (`:global([data-theme=…])`); **Acidwave** and **Surrealism** are grouped in `src/themes/<theme>.css`, which override by component class name (higher specificity than scoped styles): renaming a component class can break these themes. Since 0.4.18, what they share goes through tokens (see "Theme tokens").
- **Backgrounds** (`components/backdrops/Backdrop.svelte`): one per theme, behind the shell and the "Now playing" view. Acidwave: holographic sheets, perspective grid, halftone, grain (`AcidField`), animated only if `data-playing="true"` on `<html>`. Surrealism: hand-drawn SVG landscape (`SurrealScene`), tinted by `--album` with `oklch` mixes (in `oklab`, blue + peach gave gray); objects stay on the edges, the center is for content.
- **Accent color** (0.4.6, #23; one per theme since 0.4.104, #250): `UiSettings::accents` (`commands/settings/accent.rs`, tested), table theme → lowercase `#rrggbb`, validated on deserialization; a missing theme keeps the accent of its CSS. The old single `accent` field is only read, then stored under the theme saved with it (`migrate_accent`, in `load_from`). The UI reads `currentAccent()` (`lib/stores/ui.svelte.ts`) and `setAccent` only changes the current theme. The UI sets `--accent`, `--accent-strong` (mixed with `--text`) and `--accent-contrast` (black or white depending on luminance) as inline style on `<html>`, which wins over the theme's tokens; `null` removes them. For a theme to follow the chosen accent, its derived colors must go through `var(--accent)` (`color-mix(in srgb, var(--accent) N%, transparent)`, no hard-coded `rgb()`) and text placed on the accent through `var(--accent-contrast)`. Thumbnails (`ThemePreview`) keep their original colors. Preview: `?accent=ff5c8a`.
- **Appearance page** (0.5.40, #390): current theme block (`CurrentTheme.svelte`, `.theme-current`, star `.theme-favorite`) with a large thumbnail, description, light / dark variant and accent color (`AccentPicker`), then Animations, Size, and the gallery last (`ThemePicker.svelte`, compact `.theme-card` tiles, `.theme-filters` filters on `.cache-sizes` / `.cache-size`). Catalog in `lib/themes.ts` (tested): `scheme` mode and `family` of each theme; one tile per family (Neumorphism, Alloy), the variant is chosen in the top block, saved identifiers do not change. Click on a family: the current theme if it belongs to it, otherwise the variant of the Light / Dark filter, otherwise the one with the same mode as the current theme. Arrows move focus without applying (applying remounts the background). Favorites: `UiSettings::favorite_themes` (`commands/settings/favorites.rs`, tested), family identifiers, duplicates and unknowns ignored (no unreadable settings after going back a version), at the top of the gallery in catalog order. Thumbnails in `ThemePreview.svelte` (`small` / `large`): **a new theme** adds its thumbnail there, its entry in `THEME_INFO` (with its family if it is a variant of another) and its strings in `i18n/settings.ts`. Ruled out: dropdown list (loses the thumbnails), carousel, preview on hover (cost of the background, nothing on touch), categories (debatable boundaries; to revisit beyond 40 themes). Preview: `?favorites=alloy,terminal`.
- **Theme tokens** (0.4.18, #64, prerequisite of batch #62): the commented list is at the top of `app.css`. Besides colors: shapes (`--radius-pill`, `--radius-card`, `--radius-cover`, `--dialog-radius`, `--popover-radius`, `--control-clip`), depth (`--panel-border` / `-shadow`, `--dialog-border` / `-surface` / `-blur` / `-shadow`, `--popover-shadow`, `--shadow-cover`, `--shadow-raised`, `--shadow-inset`, `--focus-ring`), surfaces (`--control-bg`), type (`--display-style`, `--label-tracking`, `--meta-family`), motion (`--motion-fast`). Neutral values in the first `:root` block; the Seaglass block also applies to `:root`, so a token that only exists for it (`--panel-border`) must be reset to `none` by the other themes. `--radius-cover`, `--shadow-cover` and `--control-bg` are optional: without them, each element keeps its value (`var(--x, <value>)`). A new theme starts with its tokens in `app.css`; `themes/<theme>.css` only keeps the decoration (pseudo-elements, animations, special cutouts). The context menu and the three dialogs share `--dialog-*`: the update box took the frame of the other dialogs in Acidwave and Surrealism. **Checking for no visible change**: capture of the computed styles of every element, in all five themes (switching `data-theme` on the fly), over 25 preview states, stored in IndexedDB before then compared after; hover is not covered. Hidden pane: `requestAnimationFrame` does not run, wait with `setTimeout`.
- **Current track** (0.4.107, #241, `TrackRow.svelte`): the accent color on the title was not enough in some themes. The base puts on `.row.active` a 12 % accent background (18 % on hover) and an `inset 3px` accent bar; an equalizer `.now-bars` (three `i`, frozen if `player.playing` is false or `prefers-reduced-motion`) replaces the number, or sits on the cover (dark veil, hidden on hover to leave room for `.cover-play`). Aero and Alloy keep their own bar (`box-shadow`); themes that override `.row:hover` win over the background on hover (cascade below).
- **Reduced animations** (0.5.17, #351): `UiSettings::motion` = `system` (default) / `reduced` / `full`, Settings › Appearance (`.motions` / `.motion`, with `.cache-sizes` / `.cache-size`). `stores/ui.svelte.ts` sets `data-motion="reduced"` or `"full"` on `<html>` from the setting and `prefers-reduced-motion` (followed live; `motionReduced` in `lib/motion.ts`, tested). A single rule in `app.css` (`:root[data-motion="reduced"] *`, pseudo-elements included) cuts all CSS transitions and animations: **do not write `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` in a component or a theme**, the "Full" setting could not lift it; target the attribute. Progress (interpolated in JavaScript) keeps moving. Preview: `?motion=reduced|full`.
- **Cascade**: `themes/*.css` comes after the components' styles. At equal specificity (`:root[data-theme=x] .class` against `.class.svelte-x:hover`), the theme wins, including over the component's `:hover` / `.active` states: removing a theme override can therefore reactivate a hover ("Customize" and "Clear queue" in Acidwave / Surrealism since 0.4.18).
- **In-memory cache** (0.5.76, #453): `.progress .cached` band under `.fill` (width `--cached`), `color-mix` of `--text` at 22 %, stripes for Studio; it follows the shape of `.track` (masks included) without an override. `.cache` line under the Decoding node of `SignalPath`, out of the flow. A theme that redraws `.fill` can also dress `.cached`.
- **Recap** (0.5.64, #417): written on the tokens (`--panel-border`, `--panel-shadow`, `--radius-card`, `--font-display`, accent for the histograms), so dressed by each theme without an override. Classes provided for theme-specific layouts: `.recap-hero`, `.recap-total`, `.recap-highlight` (`.artist`), `.recap-kicker`, `.recap-name`, `.recap-panel`, `.recap-bars` / `.recap-bar` (`.top`), `.recap-nav`, `.recap-step`, `.recap-period`. The "Track / Artist of the period" cards keep white text on a dark veil, readable on any cover.
- **Themes**: new classes overridden by Acidwave / Surrealism: `.theme-card`, `.cache-sizes`, `.cache-size`, `.setting-row`, `.setting-switch`, `.volume`, `.mute`, `.home-customize`, `.home-editor-*`, `.queue-toggle`, `.track-tools`, `.pins`, `.pin`, `.pin-open`, `.pins-title`, `.pin-button`, `.accent-theme`, `.accent-swatch`, `.accent-custom`, `.update-check`, `.queue-clear`, `.feed-new`, `.lyrics-line`, `.lyrics-toggle`, `.lyrics-credit`, `.hires-edition`, `.hires-edition-album`, `.context-menu`, `.context-menu-item`, `.context-menu-sep`, `.queue-radio`, `.sleep-timer`, `.sleep-remaining`.
